Originals of the three G-Wishart-envelope rejection samplers as they were when the archived results in `R/results/marginal_offdiag.rds`, `R/results/partial_correlation.rds`, and `R/results/rho_uniform_calibration.rds` were produced (2026-07 to 2026-10-09).
